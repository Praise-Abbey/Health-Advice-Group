Staying Safe

Extreme Heat


Extreme Cold

  • Heat your home: Keep your main living areas and bedroom heated to at least 18°C (64°F), especially if you are elderly or vulnerable.
  • Layer your clothing: Wear multiple thin layers of clothes, a warm hat, gloves, and a scarf outdoors. Keep dry.
  • Close bedroom windows: Keep bedroom windows shut at night to lower the risk of chest infections from breathing frigid air.
  • Protect your pipes: Insulate outdoor or exposed water pipes to prevent them from freezing and bursting.
  • Drive with caution: Reduce speed, increase stopping distance, and keep an emergency kit (blanket, scraper, charged phone) in your car.
