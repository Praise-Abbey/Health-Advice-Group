What to do

Low

Air quality is good. Enjoy your usual outdoor activities.

Moderate

Most people are fine. If you have asthma or a heart or lung condition and notice symptoms, think about doing less strenuous activity outdoors.

High

Reduce strenuous activity outdoors, especially if you have a lung or heart condition or notice symptoms. Keep any reliever inhaler with you.

Very high

Avoid strenuous activity outdoors. Speak to your GP or pharmacist if you are worried about your symptoms.

This is general information, not medical advice.
